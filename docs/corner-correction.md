# Player terrain corner correction

BasePlayer.move_body() invokes Motion/CornerCorrection before move_and_slide. This resolves shallow corner opportunities before ordinary wall collision can cancel horizontal momentum. States still own when the player moves; the component has no autonomous processing.

While falling and not already grounded, two foot rays inspect the intended horizontal step for a solid top face. Recovery accepts feet already below that surface by no more than Landing Tolerance (default 4 pixels), or feet above it that cross the top during this physics step. When needed, the component raises the body to just above the top before normal motion. Handling the crossing immediately prevents a diagonal side collision from cancelling speed before the next tick, including gaps onto lower pipe tops. The raised body, projected horizontal destination, and upward path must be clear. The raised horizontal path is also swept for clearance. Recovery completes this tick's horizontal displacement above the ledge first; move_and_slide then integrates only vertical movement and registers the landing. The saved horizontal speed is restored afterward, avoiding both a duplicate horizontal step and a diagonal side collision; Air uses its normal is_on_floor transition. No separate fake grounded flag or horizontal attraction is introduced. Complete misses, deeper side contacts, and low-ceiling spaces do not receive landing correction.

While rising, a predicted ceiling collision may receive a small horizontal correction only when the projected center-head ray is unobstructed. Search up to Head Corner Tolerance (default 4 pixels), preferring travel direction, and require a clear sideways path and remaining motion. The minimum successful offset is applied, preserving velocity. A centered head obstruction or blocked escape uses the existing downward bump and sound instead. Successful corner escape continues the jump without a bump sound.

The small-Mario envelope's one-pixel foot insets place the primary rays at Body X=-5/+4, corresponding to the reference's sprite-left X=3/12 probes. Shallow recovery additionally checks just inside the physical envelope edges: Godot's full-height side sweep can hit the ledge before the inset rays cross it. This requires actual projected body overlap and keeps the same four-pixel depth limit. The reference uses a center head probe, separate side probes, landing when the foot coordinate's low nibble is below 5, and one-pixel side displacement in ImpedePlayerMove. See ChkFootMTile, LandPlyr, HeadChk, BlockBuffer_X_Adder, and ImpedePlayerMove in https://gist.github.com/1wErt3r/4048722 .

This component is a bounded adaptation for Godot's swept rectangle, not an exact port of those routines. In particular, the four-pixel horizontal ceiling escape is a project approximation of edge forgiveness, not a four-pixel push instruction in the disassembly. Current queries support unscaled, axis-aligned rectangular bodies and solid collision surfaces on the player's collision mask, including TileMap terrain and block entities. One-way platforms, slopes, moving platforms, other player sizes, and tile-specific block reactions need their own qualification.

All level visuals keep Z=0 for CanvasGroup composition. BaseLevel orders WorldObjects and then Players last on readiness, so Mario draws over terrain and blocks while decorations remain behind. The base scene also places Players after WorldObjects. Collision and visual draw order remain independent.

Regression checks cover both landing corners and rising corners, momentum preservation, centered ceiling bumps, deep wall contacts, complete misses, and low-ceiling rejection. Existing gap/ledge, jump, block, slide, air, camera-boundary, and lifecycle checks also pass.
